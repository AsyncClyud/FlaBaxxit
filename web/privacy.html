1. General Provisions

This Privacy Policy (hereinafter — the "Policy") defines the procedures for processing and protecting information about users (hereinafter — the "User") of the FlaBaxxit service (hereinafter — the "Platform"). By using the Platform, the User confirms their agreement with the terms of this Policy and the User Agreement.

2. Data Collected

The following data is processed during registration and use of the Platform:
  • Username — publicly displayed on the Platform.
  • Password — stored in the database exclusively in hashed form using industry-standard algorithms (e.g., bcrypt with a cost factor of 10); the Platform does not have access to the password in plaintext and cannot recover it.
  • Biography (Bio) — filled in at the User's discretion and is public information.
  • Account creation date — automatically recorded upon registration.
  • Published content (articles) — the text of articles posted by the User on the Platform, as well as authorship information.

    • The Platform maintains technical logs of the following events: requests for creation, modification, and deletion of data (e.g., articles), as well as registration of new accounts and authentication into existing accounts. These logs are retained for 7 days for debugging purposes and are automatically deleted thereafter. Logs are not used for user profiling and are not shared with third parties.

    Third-Party Services: We use third-party services for hosting and analytics. These providers may have access to your data but are contractually obligated to protect it in accordance with this Policy.

    3. Purposes of Data Processing

    The collected data is used exclusively for:
  • User registration and authentication;
  • Displaying the User's profile and publications to other visitors of the Platform;
  • Ensuring the functionality of the Platform (publishing, editing, and deleting articles).

    • Data is not used for advertising, analytics, or any other purposes not specified in this Policy.

    4. Legal Basis for Processing

    Data processing is carried out based on the User's consent, expressed during registration on the Platform (acceptance of this Policy and the User Agreement). For users in the European Union, processing is also based on legitimate interests and contractual necessity.

    5. Authentication and Tokens (JWT)

    For authentication, the Platform uses JWT tokens, which are transmitted via cookies. The token contains only the user identifier (userID) and expires after 24 hours. Refresh tokens are used to obtain new access tokens securely. Tokens are invalidated upon logout.

    6. Data Storage and Protection

  • Passwords are stored in the database using industry-standard hashing algorithms (e.g., bcrypt).
  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+ and at rest using AES-256 encryption.
  • Access to the database is restricted and not provided to third parties.
  • The Platform takes reasonable technical measures to protect data from unauthorized access but cannot guarantee absolute protection against all possible threats.

    • 7. Data Sharing with Third Parties

    The Platform does not share User data with third parties, except in the following cases:
  • When required by law or legal process;
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of the Platform, its users, or the public;
  • With service providers under contractual obligations to protect data.

    8. User Rights

    The User has the right to:

  • Request access to their data by emailing flabaxxit.support@atomicmail.io;
  • Modify or delete their biography and other editable profile data;
  • Delete their account, which entails the removal of profile data from the database (except where retention is required by law);
  • Request a copy of their data in a machine-readable format (data portability).
    To exercise these rights, email flabaxxit.support@atomicmail.io with the subject "Data Request." We will respond within 14 business days.

    9. Data Retention Period

    User data is stored until the User deletes their account or until the Platform's administration deletes it in cases provided for by the User Agreement. Technical logs are retained for 90 days.

    10. Data Breach Notification

    In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected users within 72 hours with prominent notice on the Platform.

    11. Age Restrictions

    The Platform is not intended for users under the age of 16. By using the Platform, you confirm you are of legal age.

    12. Policy Amendments

    The Platform reserves the right to amend this Policy. The current version is always available on the Platform. Significant changes will be communicated to Users via email or a prominent notice.
